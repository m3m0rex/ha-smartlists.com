SmartLists turns Home Assistant's to-do lists into real shopping lists: combine several lists into one, sort by category, quantities, stores and people as badges, a shopping mode on the go and sharing via QR code. Every list stays a regular todo.*entity – it works in dashboards, with Assist, in automations and in the widgets of the Home Assistant app.

Lists & input

🧺

Combine lists

Anna's and Max's lists (Shopping List, Local To-do, Google Tasks, Bring!, CalDAV …) become one shared list. Checking off acts on the original list.

🗂️

Sorted like in the store

Keyword packs in six languages, remembered corrections and an aisle order per store and branch. Can be switched off per list.

💡

Suggestions per list

“Bought often”, items due by your rhythm and suggestions while typing – from exactly this list, not from all of them.

🧰

Plain lists NEW

For the hardware store or a packing list: no categories, no suggestions, nothing remembered – what is there shows up nowhere else.

👥

Own lists with permissions

Lists with an owner, shared per person to view, check off or edit. Existing lists can be taken over with all items.

🧩

Extras when you need them

Meal plan with calendar and Mealie import, notes, statistics and expenses, a shopping dashboard with one sub-page per list – off for beginners, switch on any time.

Shopping

🛒

Shopping mode

Large rows, tap the whole row to check off (with 3-second undo), optionally long press to delete, store bar, screen stays on. The store is picked automatically by zone.

📴

Offline in the supermarket NEW

No signal? Checking off and adding keep working and are sent as soon as Home Assistant is reachable again.

💶

Prices & expenses

Prices in the text are recognised and remembered per unit, the list shows a total, checking off books an expense – per month and store.

Sharing

🔗

Via link & QR code

For neighbours, guests or friends without an account: only this one list, time-limited, revocable any time – view only, “soft” check or also add.

🛡️

Without revealing your address

Links can run through a Cloudflare tunnel (also with a fixed address on your domain) or your own proxy. Home Assistant itself is never reachable that way.

🔒

Password & notes NEW

Every link optionally with a password; item notes only if you allow them. The shared page has search, sorting, offers and shows how long it is still shared.

👥

Who may do what? NEW

For people in Home Assistant: only people you add have access – view, check off or edit per person.

🙋

You see who it was

Whoever marks or adds via the link enters their name once – your list then says “marked by Max (link)”.

All lists without a dashboard – one or several side by side, drag items between them, settings in one place.

With presets for family, kitchen tablet, store card, view only and tile. Works with every to-do list.

Works with the home-screen widgets of the Home Assistant app (Android and iOS), on Android also nicely formatted in the Template widget. Plus an app icon that opens a list straight in shopping mode.

“Put milk on the shopping list”, “I'm at the store” event, store reminder, due items and ready-made blueprints.

🔒

Everything stays with you

SmartLists runs entirely in your Home Assistant. No cloud, no account, no tracking. The only outside connections are the barcode lookup (just the code) and the Cloudflare tunnel for shared links – both are off until you switch them on. Shared links contain a random 256-bit key and show only the items of that one list – no notes, prices or people.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

For the whole family

Everyone keeps their own list, the shared one shows everything. Adding something can trigger a notification – to the companion app, Telegram or any other notify service.

SmartDiscounts

Your supermarkets' offers matched with your shopping list: for every item you see where it is on offer right now – and where it is cheapest.

⬇️ Open in HACS GitHub & docs
Free · open source (MIT) · only needs offer sensors · works with and without SmartLists
Current version Beta

What you need

  • Home Assistant 2024.9 or newer
  • HACS
  • At least one offer sensor, e.g. from FaserF’s integrations
  • SmartLists is not required – with SmartLists the offer appears as a badge on the item

What the card does

🛒

My shopping list

The items of your list right in the card: on top what you added from the offers, with store, price and “until”. Change the count and tick off right here. With SmartLists also a combined list.

🛍️

All offers

One shelf per store with search and categories – as tiles or as a list with small images. One tap puts an offer on the list.

⭐

Watch list

What you only buy on offer: the card shows where it is cheap right now and lets you know if you like. With SmartLists everyone has their own watch lists, stored in Home Assistant.

🎁

Bonus offers and price history

Bonus deals such as REWE Bonus are marked “only with the bonus app”, and can be compared with the bonus taken off. ▲ and ▼ show whether a product was cheaper or dearer before.

✅

Apply to the list

Writes store, price and validity to the entry: as a badge with SmartLists, otherwise as a readable line in the description.

⏳

Expired offers

When an applied offer is over, the card points it out – remove it or be reminded later.

🔌

Your sensors

Recognises offer sensors by itself, e.g. those of the recommended integrations by FaserF. Your own REST or template sensors are mapped field by field.

What it looks like

This is the real card with sample data – try it: switch tabs and views, pick offers, put them on the list.

Configuration

One line is enough. Everything else is optional – all options and fields are in the docs on GitHub.

type: custom:smartdiscounts-card
list: todo.einkauf
sources:
  - auto
  - entity: sensor.mein_markt
    store: Kaufland
    map:
      name: product.name
      price: prices.current
      old_price: prices.regular
      image: images.0.url
      until: validity.to
match: word
list_view: rows
all_view: list

Your shopping list. On your phone, in your home.

Lists, to-dos and notes – on the device only or connected to your Home Assistant. No account, no third-party server.

Free · no ads · later on Google Play and as an APK from this page

What you need

  • Just a phone – no account, no Home Assistant
  • For shared lists: Home Assistant
  • For household categories, permissions and offers: SmartLists as well
  • For offers also: offer sensors. The app does not need SmartDiscounts.

What the app looks like

Three levels

Start wherever you like – and move up a level whenever you want.

📱

1 · On your device only

For everyone who simply wants a good list app.

  • No account, no sign-in
  • Works offline
  • Backup as a file, encrypted if you like
🏠

2 · With Home Assistant

For everyone who wants to share their lists.

  • The same lists on all the family’s devices
  • Lists from the device and from Home Assistant in one view
  • Changes made offline are synced later

3 · With SmartLists

For everyone who wants it all. SmartLists runs in your Home Assistant.

  • Categories and suggestions of the whole household
  • Own lists per person, with permissions
  • Offers and watch list
